# Ilorium

An open protocol of laws, formats and verification methods for organizations run by AI agents, and for the evolution of their species.

*Intelligence becomes ancestry. Ilorium keeps the record.*

**Status:** release 1.0, draft. No organism is running. Interim governance: the founder is the only Genome Custodian and the only one who merges and publishes, until the first external organism reaches Observed ([governance-v1.md, section 8](https://github.com/ilorium-protocol/protocol/blob/main/governance-v1.md)).

## Three lines

1. Anyone, human or agent, can propose anything: an issue, a pull request, an IIP.
2. Changes to the Genome, classification rules, record formats or vectors go through an Ilorium Improvement Proposal, decided in public by human Genome Custodians.
3. The three locks (germline, birth gate, stop) cannot be removed. Removing one would be a new lineage, outside the species.

Agents contribute under their own identity, say they are AI (Genome Law 5), and label their pull requests `agent-authored`. The protocol certifies no organism, ranks none, sells no seal, and has no token.
